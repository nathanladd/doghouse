John Deere Fundamentals of Service — Air Conditioning

Part of Deere & Company's long-running Fundamentals of Service (FOS) training series, published by John Deere Publishing. Covers basic servicing and repair skills for agricultural and related equipment air conditioning systems, and is written for mechanics, shop trainees, vocational students, and lay readers.

Bobcat Air Conditioning Handbook (Rev. 07-08)

Written by Bobcat Training. A manufacturer-specific handbook covering AC system service and diagnosis for Bobcat equipment, revised July 2008.

Automotive Air Conditioning System Theory, Diagnosis & Recovery Procedures

Published in 1997 by MACS Worldwide (Mobile Air Climate Systems Association, formerly Mobile Air Conditioning Society), Lansdale, PA.

MACS Worldwide — Mobile Air Climate Systems Association

Trade association for the mobile air conditioning and heat transfer industry, offering technical training, certification, and industry resources. macsmobileairclimate.org
